IR Time Sync

Send the time to Sensor Watch Pro via phone screen or flashlight.

125ms (~8 bps)

Protocol

  • 8-pulse preamble (alternating white/black)
  • Start marker: 2x white
  • 6 data bytes: year (offset 2020), month, day, hour, minute, second
  • 1 checksum byte (sum of data bytes mod 256)
  • LSB first per byte, white=1 black=0
